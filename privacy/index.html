Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Rightsized is an app made by Thomas Chester ("we", "us"). It helps you understand how you see your body. Some of what you tell Rightsized is personal and health-related, so this policy explains plainly what we collect, why, and the control you have.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work for you.
  • We never sell your data, show ads, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't collect body photos, your location, your contacts, or anything about steroid, SARM or peptide use.
  • Only you can see your entries. You can delete your account and all your data in the app at any time.

Health information

Your check-ins, thought records, setup answers and measurements can reveal information about your physical or mental health. We treat all of it as sensitive. We use it only to provide the app's features to you, never for advertising, and never share it except with the service providers below that store and process it on our behalf.

Rightsized is not a healthcare provider, and we are not a "covered entity" under HIPAA. This policy, not HIPAA, describes how we handle your information. If you live in Washington, Nevada or another state with a consumer health data law, also see our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in system. Your data is stored on servers in Canada (AWS ca-central-1).
  • Email delivery for account emails is handled by our email provider.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website, and Google Fonts serves its fonts. Like any website, they receive basic technical information such as your IP address when you visit. This website doesn't use cookies or analytics.

These providers may only use your data to provide their services to us. We'll disclose information if the law requires it, for example in response to a valid legal order.

How we protect it

  • Data is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers.
  • Database access rules mean each account can only read and change its own data.
  • Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on your phone.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe and will notify you, and regulators such as the Federal Trade Commission where required, if a breach affects your data.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. To delete everything, open the app, go to Help → Delete account, and confirm. This permanently removes your account and all your entries right away. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for a short period before they are overwritten.

Your choices and rights

You can view your entries in the app at any time, and delete your account and data in the app. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request a copy of your data, correct it, or ask us questions about how it's used. To make a request, email hello@approachanalytics.io. We'll respond within 30 days.

Who Rightsized is for

Rightsized is for adults 18 and over in the United States. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app before the changes apply to you.
