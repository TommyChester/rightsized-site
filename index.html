See yourself as you really are

Body dysmorphia: feeling small no matter how much you lift. You're not the only one. Rightsized shows you where you actually stand against real population data, and helps you reflect on the thoughts that come with it.

"Body dysmorphia" here means the everyday sense, not a medical diagnosis. Rightsized doesn't diagnose, treat or test for any condition. It shows where your measurements fall in real population data.

Reality check

Guess where you rank among men your age, then measure. Rightsized compares you with CDC data from thousands of US men. The gap between your guess and the data is worth knowing.

Daily check-in

Thirty seconds to log your mood and urges, so you see the pattern instead of living inside it.

Reframe the thought

"I look small" feels like a fact. Write it down, weigh the evidence, and watch its grip loosen.
