Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

This policy is for residents of Washington, Nevada and other states with consumer health data laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act. It adds to our Privacy Policy, which covers everything in more detail.

Consumer health data we collect

  • Body measurements you enter: height, weight, arm and waist size, body fat percentage and how it was measured, and the comparisons we calculate from them.
  • Daily check-ins: mood, urge and anxiety ratings, whether you compared yourself to someone, and notes.
  • Thought records you write.
  • Setup answers that may relate to health, such as your struggles and goals and whether you see a therapist.

Where it comes from

Only from you, when you type it into the app. We don't buy health data or get it from other companies, devices or health apps.

Why we collect and use it

Only to provide the features you ask for: showing your results, trends and past entries, and suggesting next steps inside the app. We don't use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Who we share it with

We don't sell consumer health data and don't share it with third parties or affiliates. The service providers that host and process it for us (Supabase, for our database and sign-in, and our email delivery provider) act only on our instructions. We'd disclose it only if the law required it.

Consent

You consent to us collecting and using this data for the purposes above when you accept the agreement in the app. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

Your rights

  • Confirm whether we collect your consumer health data, and get a copy of it.
  • Delete it: in the app under Help → Delete account, or by emailing us.
  • Withdraw consent.
  • Appeal if we deny your request, by replying to our decision. If your appeal is denied, you can contact your state attorney general.

To make a request, email support@approachanalytics.com from the email on your account. We'll respond within 45 days.
