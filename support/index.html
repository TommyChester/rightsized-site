Support

In crisis or thinking about hurting yourself?

Call 911, or call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) in the US. You can also text HOME to 741741. Outside the US, contact your local emergency number. Our support inbox isn't monitored for emergencies.

Contact us

Email support@approachanalytics.io and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password? Open the email on the same phone and tap the link to set a new password.

I didn't get a confirmation or reset email

Check your spam or promotions folder and wait a few minutes. If it still hasn't arrived, email us from the address you signed up with.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Help → Delete account and confirm. Your account and all your entries are permanently deleted right away. See our privacy policy for details.

Why can I only do a reality check every four weeks?

Measuring often can turn into body-checking, which tends to make body worries worse. Your body also doesn't change meaningfully week to week, so a monthly check gives a truer picture.

Where do the comparison numbers come from?

From the CDC's National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (August 2021 to August 2023), which measured thousands of US men. We compare you with men in your age group. Arm size is measured relaxed, not flexed, because that's how the survey measured it.

Is Rightsized therapy?

No. Rightsized is a self-reflection tool to help you understand yourself better. It isn't medical or psychological care. If body image is affecting your life, a therapist who treats body dysmorphic disorder can help. The IOCDF directory is a good place to start.
